# Highblossom Product Context

## What we sell

Automotive glass services in Gaborone:

- Windscreen repair and replacement, including chip and crack repair
- Mobile fitting at your home or workplace, with same-day fitting
- ADAS calibration after any windscreen replacement
- Glazing for heavy machinery: cabs, windscreens and roof panels

Every job is backed by a lifetime workmanship warranty, a 24-hour response time and same-day mobile fitting.

## Who it is for

Three audiences, each with its own entry point:

- **Private drivers** with a chipped or cracked windscreen, including those claiming through insurance. Entry point: request a quote, or call or WhatsApp if the damage is urgent.
- **Fleets and businesses** that need vehicles back on the road fast, with one account and one invoice. Entry point: request a fleet account.
- **Heavy machinery operators** in mining, construction and plant hire who need cabs, windscreens and glazing fitted. Entry point: send the machine details.

## What a visitor should do

Get a free quote, then choose the route that fits. The navigation shows one primary button, **Get a Quote**. Booking an appointment belongs inside the quote flow rather than competing with it.

## Users

- **Private drivers**: a chipped or cracked windscreen, sometimes an insurance claim.
- **Fleet managers**: several vehicles, one account, one invoice, minimal downtime.
- **Plant and machinery operators**: mining, construction and plant hire equipment that cannot travel to a workshop.
- **Admin staff**: internal users managing quotes, gallery and service requests.

## Product Features

- **User Identity**: Profile management including avatar uploads and detailed account information.
- **Account Security**: Password management, 2FA, and passkey authentication.
- **Integrations**: Management of third-party service integrations (Analytics, etc.).

## Brand

- **Aesthetic**: Industrial Brutalist / Premium Dark. Raw mechanical interfaces, Swiss typography, and high-contrast dark modes.
- **Tone**: Authoritative, precise, and sophisticated. No fluff.
- **Colors**: Deep dark backgrounds (#0A0A0F), Glass surfaces (#16161D), and high-energy Red accents (#DC2626).

## Strategic Principles

- **Clarity of Craft**: Show the work. Large, high-quality images of fitted automotive glass.
- **Efficiency**: Rapid quoting and streamlined admin workflows.
- **High-End Utility**: Every interface element must feel intentional and high-performance.

## Anti-References

- Generic SaaS templates with soft gradients and rounded-full buttons.
- Overly playful or "friendly" copy.
- Cluttered, white-background layouts.
- Architectural glass and aluminium framing. It is not a service line: it has no answer to the three questions above, so it must not appear in the offer, the audiences or the entry points.
